⭐ Flagship Product

Natural Laundry Bar Soap

Our signature handmade laundry bars are crafted from plant-derived oils and locally sourced natural lye. They work powerfully on stains, ring-around-the-collar, and ground-in dirt — without synthetic fragrance, phosphates, or bleach.

  • Safe for sensitive skin and babies' clothes
  • Works in cold and warm water
  • 100% biodegradable — safe for greywater
  • Long-lasting bar — up to 40 washes per bar
  • Minimal packaging, maximum performance
