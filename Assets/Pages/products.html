Getting the Best Results

How to use your bar

1

Wet the bar and fabric

Run your bar and the stained area under water — warm water helps activate the natural oils faster, but cold works well too.

2

Rub directly onto stains

Apply the bar directly to stained areas. Work it in with your fingers or a soft brush for ground-in dirt and collar grime.

3

Lather and soak if needed

Work up a good lather. For heavily soiled items, leave the soap on for 5–10 minutes before washing.

4

Rinse thoroughly

Rinse until water runs clear. Our bars leave no chalky residue — just clean, fresh-smelling fabric.

5

Store your bar dry

Between uses, store your bar in a dry, well-ventilated soap dish. A dry bar lasts significantly longer.

What's inside every bar

Four core ingredients. All natural, all traceable, all sourced within Uganda.

🌻

Sunflower Seed Oil

Rich in linoleic acid. Creates a conditioning lather that cleans without stripping fibres.

Coconut Oil

Naturally antibacterial. Produces a dense, long-lasting foam that lifts dirt effectively.

🌿

Natural Lye (NaOH)

Locally sourced and carefully measured. Fully neutralised in the final bar — safe for skin and fabric.

💧

Purified Water

Used during saponification. Helps bind oils and lye into the stable, long-lasting bar you receive.
