Common Questions

Frequently asked

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver across Kampala and can arrange courier dispatch to other parts of Uganda. Contact us for delivery costs to your area.

How do I book a tutorial or consultancy?

The quickest way is via WhatsApp. Send us a message with your preferred dates and we'll confirm within 24 hours.

Are your soaps suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes — our bars are free from synthetic fragrances, sulfates, and parabens, making them suitable for most sensitive skin types. Always patch-test if in doubt.

Do you offer bulk / wholesale pricing?

Yes. We work with schools, hotels, and laundry businesses. Send us your quantity requirements and we'll provide a tailored quote.

How long does a bar last?

One bar handles approximately 30–40 laundry washes depending on load size and usage. Store it in a dry place between uses to maximise its life.

Can I pay via Mobile Money?

Yes — we accept MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money. Payment details are shared at checkout or via WhatsApp when you order.
