The story behind the soap

A Ugandan brand rooted in nature, community, and the belief that clean shouldn't cost the earth.

Our Mission

Cleaning homes, protecting nature

At Sunflower Laundry Soap, we believe effective cleaning doesn't require synthetic chemicals. Every bar we make is a deliberate choice to put natural ingredients first — for the health of your family, your fabrics, and Uganda's waterways.

We source ingredients from local communities across Uganda, keeping our supply chain short, our quality high, and our environmental footprint minimal. From production to packaging, sustainability is baked into everything we do.

What Drives Us

Our core values

Three principles guide every decision we make — from the ingredients we choose to the communities we serve.

Sustainability First

Every ingredient, every process, every package is evaluated for its environmental impact. We measure success not just in sales, but in how little we leave behind.

Community Over Commerce

We buy local, train local, and give back local. Supporting Ugandan farmers and entrepreneurs isn't a marketing line — it's built into our business model.

Honest Ingredients

No hidden chemicals. No misleading labels. We're transparent about what goes into our products because we believe you deserve to know exactly what touches your family's clothes.

Our Journey

How we grew

2019

The First Bar

It started in a home kitchen — experimenting with plant oils, ash, and natural lye to create a laundry bar that actually worked. That first batch was shared with neighbours and word spread.

2021

Growing the Workshop

Demand grew enough to move production out of the kitchen and into a dedicated workshop space. We standardised our recipes and began sourcing ingredients consistently from local farmers.

2022

Training & Consultancy

We formalised our soap-making tutorials and consultancy service, helping other entrepreneurs start their own natural soap businesses. A new chapter: not just making soap, but building skills.

2024–Present

Going Digital

We launched our online presence, making it possible for customers and businesses across Uganda to access our products and book consultancy sessions from anywhere.

Meet the Founder

A passion for natural living

Sunflower Laundry Soap was founded by Resty Angura — a natural soap artisan whose curiosity about plant-based cleaning grew into a full craft. What started as personal research into chemical-free living became a mission to share that knowledge with families across Uganda.

"I wanted a product that cleaned clothes the way nature intended — effectively, safely, and without leaving anything harmful behind."

Resty Angura

Founder, Sunflower Laundry Soap
