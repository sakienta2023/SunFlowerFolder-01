Our Story

Nature's answer to cleaner laundry

Sunflower Laundry Soap was born out of a simple belief — that effective cleaning doesn't require harsh chemicals. We handcraft every bar using plant-based ingredients sourced from within Uganda, keeping our production sustainable, our supply chain transparent, and our impact local.

From laundry bars for home use to training sessions for entrepreneurs, we're building a greener future one wash at a time.
